# Zentora Labs - Employee Handbook (v3.2, January 2026)

## About the Company
Zentora Labs was founded in 2019 by Maya Okonkwo and Daniel Reyes in Lisbon, Portugal.
The headquarters moved to Berlin, Germany in March 2022. As of December 2025, the company has 184 employees across 11 countries.
The company's main product is NimbusDesk, a cloud-based customer support platform.

## Working Hours
Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 Central European Time (CET). Outside core hours, employees may schedule their own time.
The standard work week is 38 hours.

## Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Fully remote contracts require written approval from the department head and the HR director (currently Priya Nair).
Remote employees receive a one-time home office stipend of 600 EUR and a monthly internet allowance of 40 EUR.

## Leave Policy
- Annual leave: 28 days per year for all full-time employees. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year.
- Sick leave: 12 paid days per year. A doctor's note is required after 3 consecutive days.
- Parental leave: 16 weeks fully paid for the primary caregiver, 6 weeks for the secondary caregiver.
- Learning budget: 1,200 EUR per employee per year for courses, books, and conferences.

## Performance Reviews
Reviews happen twice a year, in June and December. Salary adjustments are decided only after the December review and take effect on February 1.

## Expense Reimbursement
Expenses must be submitted within 30 days via the "Ledgerly" app. Business travel by train is preferred for trips under 600 km.
Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to 45 EUR per day.
