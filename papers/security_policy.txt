ZENTORA LABS - INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY (Version 2.0)

1. PASSWORDS AND ACCESS
All employee passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must be changed every 180 days.
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all internal systems. Hardware security keys (YubiKey) are issued to engineers and administrators.
Access rights are reviewed every quarter by the Security team, led by the CISO, Tomas Lindqvist.

2. DEVICES
Company laptops must use full-disk encryption. Personal devices may not be used to access production systems.
Lost or stolen devices must be reported within 2 hours to security@zentora.example.

3. INCIDENT RESPONSE
Security incidents are classified into three severity levels:
- SEV-1 (critical): customer data exposed or production fully down. Response within 15 minutes. Customers are notified within 72 hours.
- SEV-2 (high): partial outage or suspected breach. Response within 1 hour.
- SEV-3 (low): minor issue with no customer impact. Response within 1 business day.
A post-incident report must be published internally within 5 business days after any SEV-1 or SEV-2 incident.

4. DATA RETENTION
Customer ticket data is retained for 24 months after the contract ends, then permanently deleted.
Server logs are kept for 90 days. Audit logs for Enterprise customers are kept for 13 months.
Backups are performed every 6 hours and stored in two regions: Frankfurt and Dublin.

5. COMPLIANCE
Zentora Labs is certified under ISO 27001 (since 2023) and SOC 2 Type II (since 2024). The company complies with GDPR.
Annual penetration tests are performed by an external vendor in October.

6. VENDOR MANAGEMENT
New third-party vendors that process customer data require a security review by the Security team and a signed Data Processing Agreement (DPA) before use.
