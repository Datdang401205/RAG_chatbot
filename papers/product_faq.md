# NimbusDesk - Product FAQ

## What is NimbusDesk?
NimbusDesk is a cloud-based customer support platform that combines a shared inbox, live chat, a knowledge base, and an AI assistant called "Pilot".
It was first released on September 14, 2020 and the current major version is 4.1.

## Pricing Plans
| Plan | Price per agent / month | Included features |
|------|------------------------|-------------------|
| Starter | 19 USD | Shared inbox, email channel, 1 knowledge base |
| Growth | 49 USD | Everything in Starter, live chat, automation rules, 3 knowledge bases |
| Enterprise | 99 USD | Everything in Growth, Pilot AI assistant, SSO, audit logs, unlimited knowledge bases |

Annual billing gives a 20% discount. A 14-day free trial is available for Starter and Growth only. Enterprise requires a demo call.

## Limits
- Starter: up to 5 agents and 2,000 tickets per month.
- Growth: up to 50 agents and 25,000 tickets per month.
- Enterprise: no agent limit, and 200,000 tickets per month by default.
- File attachments are limited to 25 MB per file on all plans.
- The API rate limit is 100 requests per minute for Growth and 600 requests per minute for Enterprise. Starter has no API access.

## Pilot AI Assistant
Pilot drafts replies using the customer's knowledge base. It supports 14 languages, including English, German, Portuguese, Spanish, and Japanese.
Pilot never sends a reply automatically unless "auto-send" is enabled by an administrator. Pilot is only available on the Enterprise plan.

## Service Level Agreement (SLA)
Enterprise customers are guaranteed 99.95% monthly uptime. If uptime falls below this level, the customer receives a service credit of 10% of the monthly fee.
Growth customers have a 99.5% uptime target with no credits.

## Support
Starter customers get email support with a 48-hour response time. Growth customers get a 12-hour response time.
Enterprise customers get 24/7 phone support and a dedicated account manager.

## Cancellation and Refunds
Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time. Annual plans can be refunded within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, no refund is issued.
